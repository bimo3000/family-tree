Stammbaum: three mock-ups

Clickable prototypes with a fictional family. Nothing is saved: reload the page to start over. Use the dark bar at the bottom left to switch between the variants.

You can mix and match, e.g. the look of A with the cards of B and editing in a dialog.

Things to try

  • Drag the background, zoom with the mouse wheel, click Alles anzeigen.
  • Hover over a card (full dates), click a person (their line is highlighted, the panel opens), double-click to centre.
  • Karl Brandt: two marriages (his first wife died), biography, extra fields. Try + Kind: it asks "Mit wem?".
  • Peter and Sabine: divorced (the // on their line). Jonas Seidel: adopted (dashed line). The small ?: an unknown parent of two brothers.
  • Bearbeiten: type a date such as um 1850, vor 1900, März 1923 or Ostern 1923 and watch the hint and the card.
  • Delete someone, then undo with ↶ (or Ctrl+Z).

Not in the mock-ups: search (proposal P-005 is still open), real photos, saving, printing, help. The tree layout is hand-placed here, the real app computes it.
